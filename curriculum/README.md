# AppyScript Curriculum — Make Your Robot Come Alive!

Welcome! This is a 10-lesson adventure for builders aged 8–12. By the end you'll
have programmed your very own pet robot that moves, talks, senses the world and
plays games with you.

Each lesson takes about **45 minutes** and builds on the one before, so go in order.

## Curriculum Map

| # | Lesson | What you'll learn | New keywords | Time |
|---|--------|-------------------|--------------|------|
| 1 | [Hello, Appy!](lesson_01.md) | Make your robot speak and show feelings | `when start`, `say`, `show`, `show text`, `wait` | 45 min |
| 2 | [Push My Buttons](lesson_02.md) | React to buttons and shakes (events) | `when button_a pressed`, `when button_b pressed`, `when shaken` | 45 min |
| 3 | [On the Move](lesson_03.md) | Drive, turn and spin with speeds and times | `move forward`, `back`, `turn left/right`, `spin`, `stop` | 45 min |
| 4 | [Round and Round](lesson_04.md) | Repeat things with loops | `repeat ... times`, `forever`, `end` | 45 min |
| 5 | [Robot Senses](lesson_05.md) | Use distance and light sensors to make choices | `when distance <`, `when light >`, `if / else` | 45 min |
| 6 | [Robot Memory](lesson_06.md) | Store numbers in variables and count things | `let`, `set ... to`, `change ... by`, `remember` | 45 min |
| 7 | [Roll the Dice](lesson_07.md) | Random numbers and your first reaction game | `pick random ... to ...` | 45 min |
| 8 | [Keeping Score](lesson_08.md) | Lists for high scores | `list`, `add ... to`, `item ... of`, `count of` | 45 min |
| 9 | [Teach Your Robot Tricks](lesson_09.md) | Make your own commands with functions | `define`, `do`, `with` | 45 min |
| 10 | [Design Your Own Pet Robot](lesson_10.md) | The big project — put it ALL together! | everything you know! | 45 min |

## How the lessons work

Every lesson has the same friendly shape:

- **Goal** — what you'll be able to do by the end
- **New words** — the keywords you're learning, in a handy table
- **Build it** — step-by-step code that grows into one full program
- **Try it** — two challenges to make it your own
- **Watch out** — a common mistake (and the error message it causes), so you can fix it fast
- **Mini quiz** — three quick questions (the answers are printed upside-down at the bottom — no peeking!)

## What you need

- A robot or board: M5Stack (ESP32), Arduino, Raspberry Pi Pico W, or a micro:bit
- The AppyScript editor
- Your imagination!
